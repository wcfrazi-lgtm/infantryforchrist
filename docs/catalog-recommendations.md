## Merchandising

- Start with Shop all; add Hoodies, T-Shirts, Hats, and Youth collections after product types are corrected. Avoid empty categories.
- Do not label items best sellers until actual sales data supports the label. The new homepage uses “Wear what you stand for.”
- Keep youth and adult styles clearly distinguished in titles, collection cards, product copy, and size guides.
- Preserve true product colors. The new hero displays the existing product photograph without a color-changing blend effect.
- Before setting new prices, calculate garment cost, printing, supplier shipping, Shopify/payment fees, expected returns, advertising cost, and target contribution margin. Current listed prices alone do not establish profitability.
- Develop the next collection around a coherent audience and visual language. Validate concepts and samples before investing in customer acquisition.
